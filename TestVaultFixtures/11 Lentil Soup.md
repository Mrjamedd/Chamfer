# Red lentil soup

Makes about 5 bowls.

## ingredients

* 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
* 1 onion diced
* 2 carrots
* 3 cloves garlic
* 1 tsp cumin
* 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
* 5 cups vegetable stock
* lemon

## method

1. Cook onion and carrot in olive oil untill soft, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices. stir for 30 seconds.
3. Add lentils and stock then bring it to a boil.
4. Simmer 20 minutes, or until the lentils have fallen apart
5. Blend part of the soup. Add lemon, salt and pepper.

Next time: use less stock at first. Its easier to thin the soup than make it thicker.
