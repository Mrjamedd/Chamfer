# Autumn planning

I want September through November to feel less rushed than last year. The main problem wasnt having too much to do, it was starting every weekend with no idea what mattered.

## priorities

1. Finish the portfolio update before taking on another side project.
2. Keep Sunday evening mostly clear.
3. Plan visits with family early instead of trying to fit them around everything else.
4. Continue the Tuesday/Thursday exercise routine.

## possible milestones

- September: choose and edit six portfolio pieces
- october: publish the new site and ask two friends to proofread it
- November: make a simple holiday plan before travel gets expensive

There should be space for unplanned days too. A plan that fills every blank square is just a different kind of stress.
