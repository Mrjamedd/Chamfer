# What we give our attention to

Attention is often described like a spotlight, but that metaphor feels incomplete. A spotlight is controlled by one person and it points where they decide. Modern attention is more like a crowded room where everyone keeps calling your name.

I want to argue that protecting attention isnt only a productivity technique. it is a way of deciding what kind of life gets built. The difficult part is that small interruptions dont feel like decisions. Checking one message seems harmless, and then the morning has been divided into fragments.

The essay needs a better transition here. Maybe connect the personal habit to the way software is designed??

People should not have to become monks just to read a book for forty minutes. Tools can be useful without asking to be the center of every idle second. This is probably the conclusion but it arrives too suddenly and repeats the opening idea.
