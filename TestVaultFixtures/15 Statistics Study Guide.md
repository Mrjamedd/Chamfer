# Statistics midterm study guide

## Core terms

**Population:** the entire group the question is about.  
**Sample:** the observations actually collected.  
**Parameter:** a number describing a population.  
**Statistic:** a number calculated from a sample.

## things to practice

- interpret a confidence interval in words
- decide when a paired test is appropriate
- identify confounding variables
- calculate a z score without mixing up the sign
- explain correlation vs causation with an example
