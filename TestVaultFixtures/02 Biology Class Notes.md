# Cell respiration

Todays lecture was mostly about how cells turn glucose into useable energy. Glycolysis happens in the cytosol and it doesnt require oxygen. The professor said the net result is 2 ATP, not 4 because two are spent early.

## stages

1. Glycolysis
2. Pyruvate oxidation
3. Citric acid cycle
4. oxidative phosphorylation

The electron transport chain creates a proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane. ATP synthase use that gradient to produce ATP.
